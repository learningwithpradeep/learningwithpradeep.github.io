Lets Test Together: Request Deletion of Your Data

If you would like all data collected through the app "Lets Test Together" that is associated with your login ID to be deleted, please send us an email by following the steps below.

  1. Send an email to pradeepchelani@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - Lets Test Together.
  3. In the email body, mention the app name Lets Test Together and include your login ID (the email or username you use to sign in).
  4. Send the email from the address registered with your account, if possible, so we can verify your identity.
Send Deletion Request Email

Once we receive and verify your request, all data collected through Lets Test Together and linked to your login ID will be permanently deleted and you will receive a confirmation by email. This action cannot be undone.
