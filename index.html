Information Collection

We do not directly collect, store, or share any personal information from users through our applications. The applications do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

Some of our applications display ads through third-party services, which may collect certain information automatically, as described in the "Advertising" section below.

Data Storage

We do not collect or maintain personal user data on our servers. Any information or content generated while using the applications (including any files you open, create, or edit) remains on your device unless otherwise clearly stated within the application.

Advertising

Some of our applications use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information automatically, such as your device's advertising ID (for example, the Android Advertising ID), IP address, device and operating system information, and app usage data. This information is used to serve, measure, and improve ads, and may be used to show personalized ads where permitted by law and your settings.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). For more information on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other Third-Party Services

The applications may also use services provided by the Android platform and Google Play for app functionality, performance, security, or distribution. These services may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our applications, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties, except for the information that advertising partners such as Google AdMob collect automatically as described above.

Security

Since we do not collect or store personal information on our own servers, we do not maintain databases of users' personal information.
